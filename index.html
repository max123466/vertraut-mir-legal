How it works
🔐

OAuth Authorization

Each account owner authorizes access individually via TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow. No credentials are stored except access tokens.

📅

Scheduled Uploads

Pre-produced short-form videos are queued and uploaded at planned intervals with duplicate-detection across a 24-hour window.

🎵

Artist-Focused

Built exclusively for the OnlyPresi artist team. Manages official and authorized support accounts — not a public SaaS product.

🛡️

Privacy First

No TikTok user data is collected beyond OAuth tokens required for uploads. Full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
