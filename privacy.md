# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the application "vertraut mir" ("we", "our", "the App") handles user data when interacting with TikTok via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The App is a personal tool used by an individual content creator to manage and publish video content to their own social media accounts.

## 2. Data We Collect

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, we receive and store the following:

- **OAuth Access Token and Refresh Token** — used to publish content on your behalf
- **Open ID** — TikTok's unique account identifier
- **Display Name and Username** — for identification within our local interface

We do **not** collect:
- Personal information beyond the above
- TikTok content from other users
- Direct messages or private content
- Email addresses or phone numbers

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively to:
- Upload videos to your TikTok account on your authorization
- Display your account information in our local management interface
- Refresh authentication tokens automatically

## 4. Data Storage

All authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's machine in encrypted credential files. We do **not** transmit your data to any third-party servers other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties. The only data exchange happens between the App and TikTok's official API.

## 6. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are kept until you revoke access via TikTok's settings, or until the token expires. You may delete locally stored tokens at any time.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Revoke access at any time via TikTok's "Connected apps" settings
- Request deletion of locally stored data
- Be informed about how your data is used

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
